# Archive / Paintings

Local extension. Visitor mode: Experience. User delegated the selection of world-famous paintings, layout and animation. Preserve the existing archive, English controls and all collection functions. No new production dependencies.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One painting gets the room. A complete, uncropped reproduction and a readable wall label lead; a compact catalogue lets visitors choose their next work.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the existing lavender archive, cobalt controls, blue ink, Calistoga English titles and serif Chinese painting names. White image edges and a soft offset shadow distinguish the reproduction from the wall.

STORY: Browse twelve recognisable paintings, read title/artist/date and a short original looking prompt, follow museum and image sources, then inspect a full-screen reproduction.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop pairs a large artwork at left with its label at right, navigation below the label and twelve small reproductions underneath. Mobile stacks the full picture and label above a horizontally scrolling catalogue.

FORM: Local extension of the existing surface; no concept seed or identity replacement. The authored moment is a directional painted reveal with a short stagger through the wall label after the next image decodes. Full-screen viewing supports zoom and pan; reduced motion switches immediately.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
