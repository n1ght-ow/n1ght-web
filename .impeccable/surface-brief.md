## Direction contract

THESIS: An open sculpture garden of personal memories. Content sits in distinct chromatic rooms rather than a uniform paper catalogue.

OWN-WORLD: Cold white plaster, cobalt lettering, pale violet rooms, apricot workbench, dark blue readable ink. Calistoga's soft sculptural letters meet precise sans-serif controls. Tonal space replaces decorative rules.

STORY: Arrive at one large image, change the view, wander through real photographs, enter a collection room, assemble an exhibition to take away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A wide cobalt two-part wordmark above a central panoramic image with two smaller off-axis photographs. A change-view control sits beside the image caption. The bottom contains a clear collection entrance and small collection-room shortcuts, not a metric row or stock card grid.

FORM: Grounded list: exhibition catalogue, travel atlas, record sleeve, film projection, seasonal folio, memory pinboard, sculpture-garden wayfinding. Build the seventh, seed 75d82c59.
